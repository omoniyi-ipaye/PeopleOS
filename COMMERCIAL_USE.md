# Commercial use of PeopleOS

PeopleOS is publicly available as **source-available software**. You may inspect, study, modify, and use the software subject to the repository `LICENSE`.

## Commercial use requires written permission

You **must obtain express written permission from Omoniyi Ipaye before using PeopleOS for any commercial purpose**.

Commercial use includes, without limitation:

- using PeopleOS as part of a paid product, service, consultancy, managed service, or commercial offering;
- using PeopleOS to provide services to clients or other third parties;
- selling, licensing, sublicensing, hosting, or redistributing PeopleOS for commercial benefit;
- integrating PeopleOS or a derivative work into a commercial product or platform;
- using PeopleOS internally where the use forms part of a commercial service or revenue-generating offering.

If you are unsure whether your intended use is commercial, request permission before proceeding.

## Request permission

Open a **Commercial use request** using the repository issue template and describe:

- your organization;
- the intended use;
- whether PeopleOS will be modified, redistributed, hosted, or embedded;
- the expected users/customers;
- the planned commercial model.

Permission is not granted unless and until you receive **express written approval** from Omoniyi Ipaye.

## Non-commercial use

Non-commercial evaluation, learning, research, experimentation, and contribution are welcome, subject to the full `LICENSE` terms and any third-party dependency/model licenses.
